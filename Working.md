### Is_Snake Working Mechanishm
Is_Snake is a fun project based on a common belief: sometimes, your closest friends might secretly be “snakes.”

This project asks you a few questions about your friends and analyzes your answers based on common “snake-like” behaviors. At the end, your friend receives a Snake Score and is ranked based on how likely they are to be a “snake.”

Think of it as a fun way to find out which of your friends is loyal and which one might be a snake in disguise.
